How the totals are calculated

Gross proceeds = recorded sale price. Net deal profit = sale price − acquisition cost − rehab costs − closing costs − other costs. These are user-entered transaction figures, not verified accounting or tax results. Rental income remains tracked separately in Portfolio.

How rankings work

The preliminary score uses the app's existing heuristic: entered rent coverage and cash flow, ARV spread, repair ratio, estimated cash required, expenses and financing-data completeness. It does not verify listings, ownership, title, comparable sales, rents, taxes, insurance, financing availability or property condition. Missing information is shown as a diligence issue, not treated as a verified zero-cost or positive fact. A higher score is not a recommendation to buy.
