Lead verification checklist

Before treating a result as a deal, confirm the property is active, seller authority, asking price, occupancy, taxes, liens, title, condition, insurance, comparable sales, rent evidence, and whether proposed financing is actually available. Do not send an offer or pay a deposit without your approval and appropriate professional review.

Deal pipeline

Scores are screening aids, not investment recommendations, appraisals or verified underwriting. Missing or inaccurate inputs can materially change the score. Review listed risks before proceeding.
